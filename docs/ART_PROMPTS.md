# Wallpaper art: prompts and file names

The app animates on top of a painted picture: falling snow, twinkling stars in the dark sky, flickering glows on lit windows and lamps, a shooting star now and then, plus the toys and weather. Your job is just the still painting.

## How to make each image

**Best:** Midjourney. Add `--ar 16:10 --style raw --v 7` to the end of each prompt, then click **Upscale (Subtle)**.
**Also fine:** ChatGPT (image), Gemini or Ideogram. Ask for a *wide landscape* image. If the result is smaller than 2560 px wide, upscale it with the free app **Upscayl** (upscayl.org).

Rules that keep it working well with the app:

- **Wide 16:10**, at least **2560 × 1600** (2880 × 1800 or bigger is ideal for Retina Macs).
- **Bottom ~15% = calm, simple ground** (snow, sand, road). The Dock and the toys sit there.
- **Top-right corner fairly plain.** Desktop icons live there on a Mac.
- **No falling snow, rain or text painted in.** The app animates the weather itself.
- **Lit windows and lamps should be warm orange/yellow on darker surroundings.** The app finds them and makes them flicker.
- No people, logos or famous characters. You're selling this, so keep it original.
