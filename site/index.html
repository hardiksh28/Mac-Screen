Installing

  1. Open the .dmg and drag Desk Diorama into Applications.
  2. Open it. macOS says it can't verify the developer (we're a tiny indie app), so click Done.
  3. Go to System Settings → Privacy & Security, scroll down and click Open Anyway. You only do this once.
  4. Look for the snowman ☃︎ in your menu bar. Everything lives there.
It says the app "is damaged"?

Open Terminal, run xattr -cr "/Applications/Desk Diorama.app", and open it again.

Will it slow down my Mac?

It's built to be light: the wallpaper runs at 30 fps by default, everything pauses on battery and when the screen is locked, and you can switch any part off.
